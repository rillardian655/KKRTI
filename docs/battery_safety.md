# LiPo Battery Storage, Charging, and Health Guidelines

## Charging Safety Protocols

1. Always charge in a fireproof LiPo safe bag or metal ammo box.
2. Balance charge only: ensure per-cell voltage difference does not exceed 0.02V.
3. Maximum recommended charge rate: 1C (e.g. 5.0A for a 5000mAh battery).

## Storage Voltage

- Store at 3.82V to 3.85V per cell when idle for more than 48 hours.
- Never store fully charged (4.20V) or fully discharged (< 3.20V).
